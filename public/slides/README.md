# Hero Carousel Images

Place your hero slide images in this folder:

```
public/slides/
  hero-1.jpg   ← Slide 1: Children playing in a bright, colorful classroom or playground.
                 Warm, welcoming atmosphere. Shows the "Happy Beginning" vibe.
  hero-2.jpg   ← Slide 2: Students engaged in CBC learning activities — group work,
                 hands-on projects, art/craft. Shows "Excellence in Education."
  hero-3.jpg   ← Slide 3: A graduation or achievement moment — children in uniform
                 smiling, or a teacher with students. Shows "Nurturing Young Minds."
```

## Image Requirements
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:3 or 16:9 (recommended: 1200x800px or larger)
- **Style:** Bright, warm, professional school photography
- **Color palette:** Complement the school brand (deep blue #1e3a5f, gold #c9a227)

## Behavior
- Images auto-cycle every 5 seconds
- Hover over the hero section to pause
- Click arrows or dot indicators to navigate manually
- Progress bar shows time until next slide
- Images have a subtle zoom effect on hover
- Decorative frame overlay adds polish

## Tips for Great Photos
1. Use natural lighting where possible
2. Capture genuine smiles and interactions
3. Show diversity in activities (academic, sports, arts)
4. Include the school environment (buildings, playground, classrooms)
5. Update seasonally to keep the site fresh
